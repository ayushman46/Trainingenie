## Cannibalization rules

- The homepage owns the broad brand and company intent.
- The services page owns the broad programme comparison intent.
- Pillar pages own category intent.
- Individual course pages own their specific course intent.
- The blog should support commercial pages with useful education, not duplicate their sales copy.
